Hi! I'm Sam Grant, a mechanical engineer and current student at Georgia Tech. This site exists to catalog the work I've done over the years, but it's still very much a **work in progress**. 

The bulk of my time at GT has been spent on one floor of the mechanical engineering building: the one with the makerspace, machine shop, and lab/classroom for an introductory prototyping class.


Outside of school/engineering work, I love cooking (especially curries), hiking, canoeing/kayaking, and listening to music. If you're a fan of breakbeat, shoegaze, or Midwest emo, I'd love to chat.
