If a horse falls in the stable and nobody's around to see it, how do you know it's happened?

This is an ongoing project I'm working on as part of Create-X capstone, an interdisciplinary senior design class focused on creating a startup. Our team is a mix of MechE and CompSci majors

Horses are incredibly fragile and can quickly succumb to rapid-onset health conditions. For instance, colic (essentially a stomachache) is the leading cause of death in US horses - and in some cases, the window between first onset of symptoms and death is only a few hours. To save your horse's life, you need to know the instant something starts to go wrong. But what if this happens in the middle of the night?
